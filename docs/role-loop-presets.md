# Role Loop Presets

## Purpose

Four worked examples of assembling a loop from [core-interview-loop.md](core-interview-loop.md)'s 52-card bank for common role shapes, so "weight rounds to the role" (step 1 of Assembling A Loop) isn't left abstract. Adapt these, don't treat them as fixed — the actual job description and the specific decisions the person will need to make should always win over a generic preset.

Each preset lists: which rounds run, how many questions per round, which specific cards, and why. Every candidate for the *same* role in the *same* hiring cycle should still get the *same* selected questions — a preset is chosen once per role, not re-derived per candidate.

## Preset: Generalist / Growth PM

Growth roles live in Round 2 and Round 4 — the whole job is defending metrics-driven trade-offs, and growth work is exactly where the line between legitimate optimization and customer harm gets tested.

- **Round 1** (3 of 7): [The Feature That Wasn't the Problem](../Questions/behavioral/Feature-That-Wasnt-The-Problem.md), [The Loaded Ask](../Questions/situational/The-Loaded-Ask.md), [A Critique You'd Stand Behind](../Questions/judgment/A-Critique-Youd-Stand-Behind.md) — swap in [The One Deal That Needs One Feature](../Questions/situational/The-One-Deal-That-Needs-One-Feature.md) if the role sits close to sales
- **Round 2** (4 of 10, metrics-weighted): [Vanity Metrics vs. Actionable Analytics](../Questions/judgment/Vanity-Analytics_vs_Action-Metrics.md), [Deciding Before You're Certain](../Questions/judgment/Deciding-Before-Youre-Certain.md), [Shipped, But Didn't Work](../Questions/judgment/Shipped-But-Didnt-Work.md), [Ten Problems, One Focus](../Questions/judgment/Ten-Problems-One-Focus.md) — swap in [The Premortem](../Questions/situational/The-Premortem.md) if the role is launching something new and risky, [Sizing a Market You Can Defend](../Questions/method/Sizing-a-Market-You-Can-Defend.md) if the growth mandate includes new-market expansion, or [Should We Expand Into That Market?](../Questions/situational/Should-We-Expand-Into-That-Market.md) if the role's growth mandate is genuinely about entering new markets rather than optimizing an existing one.
- **Round 3** (3 of 9, skip the technical sizing add-on): [Building Trust with New Teams](../Questions/behavioral/Building-Trust-with-New-Teams.md), [Influence Without Authority](../Questions/behavioral/Influence-Without-Authority.md), [Prepping for a Room You Don't Control](../Questions/behavioral/Prepping-for-a-Room-You-Dont-Control.md) — swap in [Overruling the HiPPO](../Questions/situational/Overruling-the-HiPPO.md) if executive-driven feature requests are a real part of this role
- **Round 4** (both — this round is short by design): [A Decision You Got Wrong](../Questions/behavioral/A-Decision-You-Got-Wrong.md), [When Growth Could Harm Customers](../Questions/judgment/When-Growth-Could-Harm-Customers.md)
- **Round 5:** skip, unless the growth surface is itself AI-driven (e.g., an AI-personalized funnel) — see the AI Product PM preset below if so.

## Preset: Platform / Technical PM

Platform roles live in Round 2's delivery-and-tradeoff cards and Round 3's technical check — this candidate needs to hold their own with engineers on feasibility and sequencing, not just describe collaboration in the abstract.

- **Round 1** (3 of 5): [Assumptions Worth Testing First](../Questions/judgment/Assumptions-Worth-Testing-First.md), [Designing for a Constraint You Don't Share](../Questions/situational/AlarmClock-Design4Blind.md), [The Feature That Wasn't the Problem](../Questions/behavioral/Feature-That-Wasnt-The-Problem.md)
- **Round 2** (4 of 15, delivery-weighted): [Defending a Roadmap Trade-off](../Questions/judgment/Defending-a-Roadmap-Tradeoff.md), [Shipped, But Didn't Work](../Questions/judgment/Shipped-But-Didnt-Work.md), [Ten Problems, One Focus](../Questions/judgment/Ten-Problems-One-Focus.md), [The Premortem](../Questions/situational/The-Premortem.md) *(a platform migration or infra bet is exactly the kind of risky launch this exercise is built for)* — swap in [What Your Estimate Is Actually Measuring](../Questions/judgment/What-Your-Estimate-Is-Actually-Measuring.md) or [The 21-Point Story](../Questions/judgment/The-21-Point-Story.md) if the role owns real estimation/planning process, not just individual technical trade-offs
- **Round 3** (5 of 10, including the technical sizing check as mandatory, not optional): [Building Trust with New Teams](../Questions/behavioral/Building-Trust-with-New-Teams.md), [Influence Without Authority](../Questions/behavioral/Influence-Without-Authority.md), [Negotiating the Technical-Debt Fire](../Questions/situational/Negotiating-the-Technical-Debt-Fire.md), [Dealing with Seismic Paradigm Shifts](../Questions/behavioral/Dealing-with-Seismic-Paradigm-Shifts.md), [Technical Sizing Sanity Check](../Questions/method/technicalSizing.01.md) *(run by or with an engineer)* — swap in [Who Actually Owns This Decision](../Questions/judgment/Who-Actually-Owns-This-Decision.md) if engineering-vs-design ownership disputes are common for this role
- **Round 4** (both): [A Decision You Got Wrong](../Questions/behavioral/A-Decision-You-Got-Wrong.md), [When Growth Could Harm Customers](../Questions/judgment/When-Growth-Could-Harm-Customers.md)
- **Round 5:** skip, unless the platform itself is an AI/ML platform other teams build on — see the AI Product PM preset below if so.

## Preset: AI Product PM

The only preset where Round 5 is mandatory rather than optional — see [ai-competency-model.md](ai-competency-model.md) before using it at all, and skip this whole preset for a role where AI isn't a real part of the product surface.

- **Round 1** (2 of 5): [Feature That Wasn't the Problem](../Questions/behavioral/Feature-That-Wasnt-The-Problem.md), [Assumptions Worth Testing First](../Questions/judgment/Assumptions-Worth-Testing-First.md)
- **Round 2** (3 of 10, AI-adjacent): [Where AI Actually Helps](../Questions/ai/Where-AI-Actually-Helps.md), [Build, Buy, or Wait](../Questions/ai/Build-Buy-or-Wait.md), [AI as a Moat, or Not](../Questions/ai/AI-as-a-Moat-or-Not.md)
- **Round 3** (2 of 5): [Influence Without Authority](../Questions/behavioral/Influence-Without-Authority.md), [Prepping for a Room You Don't Control](../Questions/behavioral/Prepping-for-a-Room-You-Dont-Control.md)
- **Round 4** (both, one AI-flavored): [When Growth Could Harm Customers](../Questions/judgment/When-Growth-Could-Harm-Customers.md), [The Line You Wouldn't Cross](../Questions/ai/The-Line-You-Wouldnt-Cross.md) *(from the Round 5 pool — appropriate here since it's the AI-specific ethics card)*
- **Round 5** (mandatory, 4 of 12): [Explaining AI Limits to a Skeptic](../Questions/ai/Explaining-AI-Limits-to-a-Skeptic.md), [How Much Should the AI Decide](../Questions/ai/How-Much-Should-the-AI-Decide.md), [When the Model Is Wrong](../Questions/ai/When-the-Model-Is-Wrong.md), [What the Model Needs to Know](../Questions/ai/What-the-Model-Needs-to-Know.md) — swap in [Governing an Agent That Acts on Its Own](../Questions/ai/Governing-an-Agent-That-Acts-on-Its-Own.md) if the product involves multi-step autonomous agents rather than single-turn AI features, and [Scanning External Forces Before You Bet on AI](../Questions/ai/Scanning-External-Forces-Before-You-Bet-on-AI.md) if the role sits in a heavily regulated or fast-moving-competitive space

Note this preset uses fewer questions in Rounds 1-3 than the others specifically to make room for Round 5 — the goal is 3-4 questions per interviewer per round, not more rounds crammed into the same time.

## Preset: Director / VP-Level (Any Domain)

Layer this on top of whichever domain preset above fits the role — it changes *how* the loop is run, not *which* cards. Apply the pressure guidance in [evidence-and-pressure-standard.md](evidence-and-pressure-standard.md) and use the Director/VP columns in [interview-scorecard-template.md](interview-scorecard-template.md)'s benchmark tables, which expect higher scores across most competencies at this level.

- **Prioritize cards with a Role Or Level Notes section**, since they carry a senior-specific follow-up or an explicit pressure note: [Building Trust with New Teams](../Questions/behavioral/Building-Trust-with-New-Teams.md), [Influence Without Authority](../Questions/behavioral/Influence-Without-Authority.md), [Reading Market Signals](../Questions/judgment/Reading-Market-Signals.md), [Defending a Roadmap Trade-off](../Questions/judgment/Defending-a-Roadmap-Tradeoff.md).
- **Raise pressure to `resistant`** (never `hostile-room` — see the Candidate Safety Cap in [evidence-and-pressure-standard.md](evidence-and-pressure-standard.md)) on Round 2 and Round 3 specifically — these are where a Director/VP candidate should be expected to hold up under real scrutiny, not just tell a clean story. [The Skeptical Engineer](interviewer-voices.md#the-skeptical-engineer) fits Round 2 well; [The Time-Pressed Executive](interviewer-voices.md#the-time-pressed-executive) fits Round 3's [Prepping for a Room You Don't Control](../Questions/behavioral/Prepping-for-a-Room-You-Dont-Control.md) particularly well, since the card is already about handling that exact pressure.
- **Round 4 matters more, not less, at this level** — a Director/VP candidate sets the ethical tone for people below them; don't shorten this round to make room elsewhere. Keep [The Accountability Partner](interviewer-voices.md#the-accountability-partner)'s pressure at `skeptical`, not `resistant` — this round is about depth, not resistance, even for senior candidates.
- **Add Round 6 only if the role actually manages other PMs.** Not every Director/VP-level role does — a Principal or Staff-equivalent IC track doesn't. If it does, run all four [people-management](people-management-competency.md) cards; this round doesn't need trimming the way Round 2 or Round 5 does.

## Maintaining This Document

Add a new preset when a role shape recurs often enough to be worth naming, rather than re-deriving the same weighting from scratch each time. Update an existing preset if a new card is added to the bank that clearly belongs in it — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
