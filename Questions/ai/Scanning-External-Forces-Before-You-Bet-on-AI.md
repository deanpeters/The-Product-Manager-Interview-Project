# Scanning External Forces Before You Bet on AI

> Tell me about an external force — regulatory, economic, social, or competitive — that changed your plan for an AI feature or product, before or after you'd shipped it.

> **Follow-up** What's a signal you watch on an ongoing basis for this, rather than something you only checked once at the start?

## Type

Judgment

## Competencies Assessed

- Product Judgment, Strategy, And Prioritization

## AI Competency

[AI Risk, Safety & Responsible Use](../../docs/ai-competency-model.md#ai-risk-safety--responsible-use) (also relevant to [AI Strategy & Competitive Advantage](../../docs/ai-competency-model.md#ai-strategy--competitive-advantage))

## Why This Matters

Only use this card for roles where AI is a real part of the product surface — see [ai-competency-model.md](../../docs/ai-competency-model.md). AI products are unusually exposed to forces outside the product team's control — shifting regulation, public controversy over similar products, sudden cost changes in the underlying models, competitive moves. This card is distinct from [The Line You Wouldn't Cross](The-Line-You-Wouldnt-Cross.md) (which tests internally-discovered risk) and [Separating a Funded Move From a Narrated One](../judgment/Separating-a-Funded-Move-From-a-Narrated-One.md) (which tests skepticism toward a specific competitor claim) — this one tests whether a candidate scans the broader external environment at all, and keeps scanning rather than checking once.

## Neutral Follow-Up Probes

- What specifically changed as a result — scope, timeline, disclosure, data handling, something else?
- How did you first learn about the external force — did you have a process for finding it, or did it find you?
- Who else needed to know about this shift?

## Strong Evidence

1. Names a specific, real external force — a named regulation, a real market or pricing shift, a genuine public reaction to a comparable AI product — not a generic "the landscape is changing" statement.
2. Describes a concrete, real change to the plan as a result — not a cosmetic acknowledgment.
3. Shows the force was caught through some active scanning process, not pure luck.
4. In the follow-up, names a real ongoing signal being monitored, not a one-time check performed at project kickoff.

## Warning Signs

1. No specific external force named, or the answer stays at the level of general industry commentary.
2. The described "change" didn't actually alter anything concrete.
3. No ongoing monitoring — external scanning was a one-time exercise, if it happened at all.

## Scoring Anchors

- **5:** Specific real external force, a concrete resulting change, evidence of active (not lucky) discovery, and a genuine ongoing monitoring signal.
- **3:** A real force is named but the resulting change or the monitoring practice is thin.
- **1:** No specific force, or no real change resulted from it.
