# Mid-Level Product Manager Interview Question Catalog

## Executive Recommendation

Use a **100-question master bank**, not a 100-question interview.

Recommended weighting:

- 80% behaviors, judgment, mindset, and interpersonal effectiveness
- 20% product methods and execution techniques
- Prefer evidence from actual past behavior, supplemented by situational and product-judgment prompts

---

# Competency Framework

The literature converges around four reinforcing PM responsibilities:

1. **Understand** customers, needs, alternatives, context, and evidence.
2. **Decide** what matters, what not to do, which risks to accept, and how to balance customer and business value.
3. **Mobilize** people through influence, communication, shared understanding, and trust.
4. **Learn and Deliver** through ownership, experimentation, measurement, adaptation, and execution.

# Recommended Interview Coverage Model

| Interview | Suggested Sampling | Core Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Customer and Product Judgment | 2 customer, 3 judgment, 1 ethics | Finding and protecting value |
| Influence and Communication | 3 collaboration, 2 communication, 1 leadership | Mobilizing others |
| Strategy and Ambiguity | 2 strategy, 2 ambiguity, 1 ownership, 1 learning | Independent decision-making |
| Applied Product Practice | 2 discovery, 1 metrics, 1 roadmap, 1 delivery | Practical PM execution |

## Sampling Rule

For a standard interview loop:

- 12–14 behavioral questions
- 4–5 situational questions
- 3–4 product judgment questions
- 3–4 technique questions

Probe deeply into:
- Context
- Candidate’s personal contribution
- Alternatives considered
- Stakeholders affected
- Evidence used
- Outcome achieved
- Learning and adaptation

The goal is to evaluate demonstrated judgment, not framework memorization.
