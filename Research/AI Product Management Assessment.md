# AI Product Management Assessment

An internal AI-PM competency and leveling framework contributed to this project, in the same spirit as [`Product Manager Assessment - December 11, 2021.csv`](Product%20Manager%20Assessment%20-%20December%2011%2C%202021.csv). It names ten AI-specific PM competencies and benchmarks each 1-5 by role level (Product Manager / Senior PM / Director / VP).

This is a proprietary worksheet, not externally cited research — treat it as a starting default to calibrate against your own org, not a validated external standard. It directly informed [`docs/ai-competency-model.md`](../docs/ai-competency-model.md), the AI-PM section of [`docs/interview-scorecard-template.md`](../docs/interview-scorecard-template.md), and the ten AI-competency interview cards indexed in [`docs/question-index.md`](../docs/question-index.md).

| AI Product Management Category | What are we assessing? | Product Manager | Sr | Dir | VP |
| --- | --- | ---: | --: | --: | --: |
| **AI Fundamentals** | Understands capabilities, limitations, probabilistic behavior, hallucination, context, models, and basic AI/ML concepts | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **AI Opportunity Discovery** | Identifies where AI can meaningfully improve customer outcomes, workflows, decisions, or economics rather than simply adding "AI" | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| **AI Product Discovery & Validation** | Tests AI use cases, desirability, feasibility, uncertainty, and assumptions before committing to production | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| **Data & Context Strategy** | Understands what data, knowledge, context, retrieval, feedback, and permissions the AI product requires | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **AI Experience & Human Agency** | Designs appropriate interaction between AI and humans, including augmentation, automation, oversight, confidence, and escalation | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| **AI Evaluation & Metrics** | Defines how AI quality and product outcomes are evaluated, including task success, accuracy, reliability, cost, latency, and behavioral consequences | 2 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| **AI Risk, Safety & Responsible Use** | Recognizes and manages privacy, security, bias, explainability, compliance, misuse, and inappropriate delegation of agency | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| **AI Economics & Technical Tradeoffs** | Understands model choice, build/buy/partner decisions, inference cost, latency, scalability, and quality/cost tradeoffs | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| **AI Product Operations & Learning** | Uses telemetry, evaluation, feedback, monitoring, and changing model behavior to continuously improve the product | 2 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| **AI Strategy & Competitive Advantage** | Connects AI capabilities to product strategy, differentiation, defensibility, business model, and portfolio choices | 2 | 3 | 5 | 5 |
