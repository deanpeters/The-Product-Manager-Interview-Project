# The Premortem

> Imagine it's one year from now, and the initiative you're most excited about right now has completely failed. Walk me through why.

> **Follow-up** Which of those failure causes is actually most likely to happen? What would you start doing today because of it?

## Type

Situational

## Competencies Assessed

- Product Judgment, Strategy, And Prioritization

## Why This Matters

A premortem — imagining failure as already having happened, then working backward to the causes — surfaces risks that forward-looking planning tends to hide, because it removes the psychological pressure to defend the plan. This card tests whether a candidate can generate genuinely specific failure modes rather than hedged generalities, and whether they'll act on the most credible one rather than treat the exercise as a formality.

## Neutral Follow-Up Probes

- Be specific — what exactly went wrong, and for whom?
- Is that a risk you're already tracking, or one this exercise just surfaced?
- What would you need to see in the next month to know you're heading toward that failure?

## Strong Evidence

1. Generates multiple distinct, specific failure causes — not a single vague "it could fail if things don't go well."
2. Doesn't hedge or defend the current plan mid-exercise; genuinely inhabits the "it already failed" frame.
3. Includes at least one failure cause that isn't purely external (market, competitor) — something within the team's or candidate's own control.
4. In the follow-up, triages the causes by real likelihood rather than treating them as equally probable, and names a concrete action starting now, not just "I'd keep an eye on it."

## Warning Signs

1. Only generates one generic failure cause, or hedges it back into "but that probably won't happen."
2. All failure causes are external and out of the candidate's control — no ownership of internal risk.
3. Follow-up produces no real triage or concrete action — the exercise stays hypothetical.

## Scoring Anchors

- **5:** Multiple specific, non-hedged failure causes including at least one internal one, real triage of likelihood, and a concrete action started today.
- **3:** Some real failure causes generated, but they're generic or the follow-up triage/action is vague.
- **1:** One vague, hedged failure cause, or no real engagement with the hypothetical.
